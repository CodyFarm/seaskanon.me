1. compulsory education — 义务教育
   > 例句：Compulsory education should be provided free of charge to ensure equal opportunities for all children.
   （同义：mandatory education, required education）
   （近义：强制教育，普及教育）
   💡 compulsory ← com-(共同) + puls(推动) + -ory(形容词后缀)，意为“强制性的”。

2. vocational education — 职业教育
   > 例句：Vocational education equips students with practical skills that are directly applicable to the job market.
   （同义：technical education, career training）
   （近义：职业培训，技术教育）
   💡 vocational ← vocation(职业) + -al(形容词后缀)，源自拉丁语 vocatio(召唤)。

3. academic performance — 学业表现
   > 例句：Students’ academic performance is often influenced by their motivation and study habits.
   （同义：scholastic achievement, educational attainment）
   （近义：学习成绩，学术成就）

4. non-academic affairs — 与学习无关的事情
   > 例句：Schools should also address non-academic affairs such as students’ mental health and social skills.
   （同义：extracurricular matters, non-scholastic activities）
   （近义：非学业事务，课外事务）

5. puppy/ calf love — 早恋
   > 例句：Puppy love, though often transient, can distract teenagers from their studies.
   （同义：adolescent romance, teenage crush）
   （近义：青春期恋情，初恋）

6. corporal punishment — 体罚
   > 例句：Corporal punishment has been banned in many countries as it can cause both physical and psychological harm.
   （同义：physical punishment, bodily chastisement）
   （近义：身体惩罚，肉刑）
   💡 corporal ← corpus(身体) + -al(形容词后缀)，意为“身体的”。

7. tuition fee — 学费
   > 例句：The rising tuition fees have become a heavy burden for many families.
   （同义：school fees, educational expenses）
   （近义：学杂费，教育费用）

8. parents’ supervision — 父母的监督
   > 例句：Parents’ supervision is crucial in preventing children from developing bad habits online.
   （同义：parental guidance, parental oversight）
   （近义：家长管教，父母监管）
   💡 supervision ← super-(在上面) + vision(看)，意为“从上往下看”，即监督。

9. individual tutorship — 个人辅导
   > 例句：Individual tutorship can help struggling students catch up with their peers in specific subjects.
   （同义：one-on-one coaching, private tutoring）
   （近义：一对一辅导，家教辅导）

10. turning point — 转折点
    > 例句：The decision to study abroad became a turning point in her academic career.
    （同义：critical moment, watershed moment）
    （近义：关键节点，分水岭）

11. the pursuit of knowledge — 对知识的追求
    > 例句：The pursuit of knowledge should be the primary goal of higher education.
    （同义：the quest for knowledge, the search for truth）
    （近义：求知，探索知识）

12. maintain / disrupt classroom order — 维护、扰乱课堂秩序
    > 例句：Teachers must establish clear rules to maintain classroom order, while disruptive behaviour should be addressed promptly.
    （同义：keep / break classroom discipline）
    （近义：维持/打破课堂纪律）

13. coeducational /single sex establishment — 男女共校、单一性别学校
    > 例句：Coeducational establishments are believed to better prepare students for mixed-gender workplaces, whereas single-sex schools may reduce distractions.
    （同义：mixed-school / single-gender school）
    （近义：男女合校 / 男校或女校）
    💡 coeducational ← co-(共同) + educational(教育的)。

14. generation gap — 代沟
    > 例句：The generation gap between parents and teenagers often leads to conflicts over lifestyle choices.
    （同义：generational divide, age gap）
    （近义：代际差异）

15. indulge in — 沉迷 (备注: 表示“溺爱” 后面直接加宾语)
    > 例句：Some teenagers indulge in video games, which negatively affects their academic performance.
    （同义：be addicted to, immerse oneself in）
    （近义：沉溺于，热衷于）

16. play truant — 逃学
    > 例句：Students who play truant are at a higher risk of falling behind and dropping out.
    （同义：skip school, ditch class）
    （近义：旷课，翘课）
    💡 truant 源自法语，意为“流浪者”。

17. school shootings — 校园枪击
    > 例句：School shootings have raised serious concerns about campus safety and gun control.
    （同义：campus shootings, school violence）
    （近义：校园枪击案）

18. broaden one’s horizons — 开阔视野
    > 例句：Traveling and reading widely can help young people broaden their horizons and develop open-mindedness.
    （同义：expand one’s outlook, widen one’s perspective）
    （近义：拓展眼界，增长见识）

19. the hardship and bitterness of life — 生活的艰辛和苦难
    > 例句：Experiencing the hardship and bitterness of life can teach young people resilience and appreciation.
    （同义：the struggles and sufferings of life）
    （近义：生活的艰难与痛苦）

20. appropriate advice and guidance — 恰当的建议和指导
    > 例句：Parents and teachers should provide appropriate advice and guidance to help teenagers make wise decisions.
    （同义：suitable counsel and direction）
    （近义：合理的建议和引导）

21. cultivate independence — 培养独立性
    > 例句：Boarding schools aim to cultivate independence by encouraging students to manage their own daily routines.
    （同义：foster self-reliance, nurture autonomy）
    （近义：培养自立，培养自主性）

22. a good habit of frugality — 节约的好习惯
    > 例句：Parents should teach their children a good habit of frugality from an early age to prepare them for financial responsibility.
    （同义：a habit of thrift, a practice of economizing）
    （近义：节俭的好习惯，勤俭节约）
    💡 frugality ← frugal(节约的) + -ity(名词后缀)。

23. distinguish right from wrong — 明辨是非
    > 例句：One of the main goals of moral education is to help children distinguish right from wrong.
    （同义：tell right from wrong, discern good from evil）
    （近义：分辨对错，辨别是非）

24. shoulder the responsibility of — 肩负…… 的责任
    > 例句：Both parents and schools must shoulder the responsibility of educating the younger generation.
    （同义：take on the responsibility of, bear the burden of）
    （近义：承担责任，担负……的责任）

25. be prone to do — 容易做某事
    > 例句：Teenagers are prone to make impulsive decisions without fully considering the consequences.
    （同义：be inclined to do, be susceptible to doing）
    （近义：倾向于做，易于做）

26. go astray — 误入歧途
    > 例句：Without proper guidance, young people may go astray and engage in delinquent behaviour.
    （同义：lose one’s way, fall into bad habits）
    （近义：走错路，步入歧途）

27. violate social conventions and laws — 违反社会习俗和法律
    > 例句：Juvenile offenders often violate social conventions and laws due to a lack of moral education.
    （同义：break social norms and legal rules）
    （近义：违背社会规范和法律规定）

28. cast a shadow on one’s heart — 在心中留下阴影
    > 例句：The experience of bullying can cast a shadow on a child’s heart and affect their self-esteem for years.
    （同义：leave a scar on one’s psyche, cause emotional trauma）
    （近义：给心灵留下阴影，造成心理创伤）

29. resist the temptation of — 抵制……的诱惑
    > 例句：It is important for adolescents to learn how to resist the temptation of peer pressure and risky behaviours.
    （同义：withstand the lure of, fight the attraction of）
    （近义：抵挡……的诱惑）

30. interpersonal skills — 人际交往的技巧
    > 例句：Team projects in school help students develop essential interpersonal skills for the workplace.
    （同义：social skills, communication abilities）
    （近义：社交技巧，人际沟通能力）

31. peer pressure — 同辈的压力
    > 例句：Peer pressure can have both positive and negative effects on teenagers’ behaviour and choices.
    （同义：social pressure from peers, group influence）
    （近义：同龄人压力，群体压力）

32. formative years — 性格形成期
    > 例句：What children learn during their formative years often shapes their lifelong values and habits.
    （同义：developmental years, crucial childhood years）
    （近义：成长关键期，性格塑造期）
    💡 formative ← form(形成) + -ative(形容词后缀)。

33. physically and emotionally immature — 身心不成熟
    > 例句：Teenagers are often physically and emotionally immature, which makes them vulnerable to negative influences.
    （同义：inexperienced in body and mind, not fully developed）
    （近义：身心发育不全，不够成熟）

34. a sense of superiority — 优越感
    > 例句：Constant praise from parents may give children a false sense of superiority.
    （同义：feeling of superiority, superiority complex）
    （近义：优越心态，自大感）

35. socially adaptable — 能适应社会的
    > 例句：Education should aim to produce socially adaptable individuals who can thrive in a changing world.
    （同义：socially flexible, well-adjusted to society）
    （近义：适应社会能力强的）

36. adjust teaching methods — 调整教学方法
    > 例句：Teachers should adjust teaching methods to cater to the diverse learning needs of students.
    （同义：modify instructional approaches, adapt teaching strategies）
    （近义：改变教学方式，灵活教学）
